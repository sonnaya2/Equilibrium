# Team Crystal — Crystal Frontier

Locked borders as living crystal-ivy; unlocked land sun-warmed stone; facet pin tray.

## Thesis

High-fantasy wartable where **gem lives only on edges** — vines, pin rims, selected plinth facet, chrome. Terrain stays stone: warm grain for unlocked, dusk for locked/dim. Selection lifts one slab and dims the field. Pins open a **facet-chip tray** docked at the board foot (content + drops from fixture), never a permanent side inspector.

## Must sell

1. **Brightness** — readable stone grain at rest (not mud void).
2. **Selection** — elevated + brightest subject; others dusk-dim; stay elevated while selected.
3. **Crystal-ivy** — faceted/shard vines on **land borders between regions**, pulse/growth — not viewport corner ribbons.
4. **Water** — darker abyss + bright moving caustic streaks (CSS only, cheap).
5. **Pin dossier** — marker → facet tray with content + drops (honest empty drops).
6. **UI shell** — Board Sky: board primary, thin crest ledger under; transient tray OK; no third column.
7. **Anti-slop** — Editorial tokens; gem chrome only; no neon SaaS / crypto glass.

## Risk avoided

Gem never floods terrain. No rainbow glow. No permanent inspector rail. No photo plate.
