## What ships

### Champion DNA — Cartographer's Desk

**Ships as the planning language.**

- **Parchment land + ink coasts** — brightest rest state in the field. Labels sit on paper, not mud. Selection is thick ink outline + moderate plinth lift + paper halo — **zero mint wash**. That alone beats half of production-era dark boards.
- **Three-band desk under a ~70% board** — Regions ledger (crests) → parchment place slip (content | drops) → sources strip. This is the wiki-tool stack the product already promised. No permanent side inspector. Fixture tags and honest empties are done correctly.
- **Cartographic water** — hatch + animated wave polylines. Not teal glow blobs. Reads as charted sea. Cheap, legible, on-brand for a planner.
- **Rope-and-ivy on land coasts** — west / south / north / east borders, not viewport ribbons. Plant language is real (brown rope + green leaves). Still stroke-weight volume, not Raised-class mass — hence 7.5 vines, not 9.

**Dies / fix before production:** Clear picks is cosmetic (no real state toggle). Rope vines need extrusion mass when they hit WebGPU. Compass is fine as decoration; do not let chrome sprawl.

### Runner-up DNA — Deep Board Sky

**Ships as the materials baseline.**

- True board-majority + under-strip ledger + dossier. Purest Hybrid stack fidelity. Slabs have cliff faces, top-lit grain, dual-rim focus without flooding the cap mint.
- **Water craft wins the field** — multi-band directional swell + foam ridges + glint. This is what “animated sea that reads as water” means in CSS.
- Triple-stroke seam tubes (shade / core / highlight) on real inter-slab paths. Sparse leaves hurt plant density.
- Working elective pick model + Clear picks. Pin → map-chip dossier with content/drops and region stub fallback. Fixture-honest sources line.
- Missing full product nav chrome is a lab choice, not a crime — but production still needs EQUILIBRIUM + six-link nav around this stack.

### Steal from Raised Court (not champion)

- **Selection theatre:** `translate3d(0, -42px, 40px) scale(1.06)` + thick plinth foot + spotlight. Nobody else makes “this region is on stage” this obvious. Boardsky/Daylit’s −10px lift is polite; Raised is memorable.
- **Volume hedges:** trunk extrusion + leaf clusters + open-court recession when both sides unlocked. Only team that would survive a hardass “flat ribbon = hard fail” audit without sweating.
- Stele pins and floating bottom dossier bar are valid **transient** patterns — keep as pin UX option, not as a replacement for under-board region stub.

**Do not ship Raised whole:** ledger-above-board + dark reflective sea is a codename win and a Board Sky purity loss. Overview brightness is OK by design, but focus-mode recessed land at ~0.58 brightness flirts with mud. Default product light should stay closer to carto/boardsky.

## Team autopsies (brutal)

### 1. cartographer — 8.94 · CHAMPION (under bar)

You built the only mock where a still frame answers “what do I pick and what is at this pin.” Ink coasts kill soft fog. Desk bands kill the side-inspector temptation. Fixture honesty is product-grade.

You did **not** hit 9.0 because vines are still elegant rope drawings and water is a chart, not a war-table ocean. Remaster of *materials* still needs Boardsky/Raised blood. Clear picks that does nothing is junior. Fix it.

### 2. boardsky — 8.88 · runner-up

If the only question is “does this match Hybrid Board Sky DNA,” you win. Water and slab craft are tournament-best materials. Vines are correct tubes on correct seams with too few leaves. You lost the crown by **0.06** to atlas readability — parchment labels beat umber grain for screenshot planning. Ship your stack; borrow carto land wash for rest-state labels.

### 3. raised — 8.50 · drama MVP

Selection and vine volume own the room. A player remembers “the raised court.” Production should steal plinth height and hedge mass **into** Board Sky, not replace the under-board desk with ledger-on-top + dark abyss as default. Floating dossier is fine; permanent darkness is not.

### 4. daylit — 8.31 · competent mid

Everything present, nothing dominant. Noon thesis is real. Selection is gem-rim plinth without mint flood. Dossier tables are dense and honest. Vines/water sit in the “good enough CSS demo” band. Participation is not a trophy; this is fourth.

### 5. crystal — 7.75 · last

Thesis discipline (gem on edges only for terrain) is respected. Execution of water and plant-read is not. Shard ivy on seams is clever and still not vegetation. Facet tray is the keep. Crystal Frontier does not become the map.

## Production path (binding recommendation)

1. **Layout:** Boardsky stack — full-width board majority; horizontal crest ledger under; region stub + pin map-chip dossier under; zero side inspector.
2. **Land at rest:** Cartographer parchment lift + ink coast language on authored slabs (not photo plate). Labels must survive a grayscale screenshot.
3. **Selection:** Raised lift magnitude (or Boardsky −10px minimum with taller cliff) + Boardsky dual gem **rim only** — never mint fill. Stay elevated while selected.
4. **Vines:** Raised volume hedges on locked frontiers; Boardsky triple-stroke tubes on open seams; carto rope-ivy only if WebGPU plant budget is thin. Never viewport corners.
5. **Water:** Boardsky multi-band swell + foam as default; carto hatch as optional “atlas mode” if you ever dual-skin (you probably should not dual-skin for v1).
6. **Pins:** Boardsky/Daylit pin → under-board content|drops; Crystal facet chips optional for dense multi-row content; Raised stele silhouette only if markers need height over 3D slabs.
7. **Honesty:** Fixture labels, honest empties, sources strip — carto band 3 is the template.

**Pass bar remains 9.0.** R1 champion is **cartographer at 8.94**. Call it a provisional crown: production remaster is not “done” until vine volume and live materials clear the bar the lab could not.
