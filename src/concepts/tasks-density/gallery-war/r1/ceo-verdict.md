## SCREAMS (R2 must-fix for survivors)

1. **Nobody is at 9.2.** Stop self-scoring 9.x on craft. Production needs a composite that beats Herald on *scan density + full-set ops + no chrome tax*, not one gimmick each.

2. **`slice(0, 120)` is a shared lie.** Only Crucible refuses it. Cipher / Quill / Sigil / Grove must either absorb virt or prove filters make 120 honest. Cap without virt is not “ops parity.”

3. **Cipher owns scan. Crucible owns reach. Sigil owns detail width. Grove owns column count. Quill owns chrome height.** R2 survivors who do not *steal* from dead peers will plateau at 8.9 forever.

4. **Kill foot redundancy.** Region in meta *and* foot is Herald waste. Ash got this right. Ember / Crucible / Quill / Sigil still litter.

5. **Idle gem chrome is a kill condition.** Ember died for it. Survivors with rest-state multi-stop washes and outer blurs (Crucible tiles, Sigil tiles) will get cut harder next round if scan doesn’t justify them.

6. **Viewport fill ≠ shell height alone.** Quill’s track is correct; board minmax still Herald. Grove’s columns are correct; names get crushed. Both axes must rise together.

7. **In-tile expand is still a postage stamp** for long descriptions. Sigil’s band is the only structural answer. Cipher’s ribbon does not excuse a cramped detail body if you keep in-tile.

8. **Do not reintroduce Bastion’s 2-col fantasy or Vault’s crest monument.** Those are dead bets. Steal their *readable name size* and *region identity*, not their void.
